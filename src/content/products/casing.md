## Overview

We supply seamless casing manufactured to API 5CT / ISO 11960 for every string in the well: conductor, surface, intermediate and production. Each order is placed with a mill selected for the required size, grade and PSL, and is supplied with full mill test documentation.

Typical enquiries cover standard carbon grades for surface and intermediate strings, sour-service grades (L80, C90, T95) qualified to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156, and high-strength P110 and Q125 for deep production strings.

## End finish

- Threaded and coupled (T&C), coupling power-tight on one end
- Plain end (PE) for welding or field threading
- Special drift, special clearance couplings and special bevel on request

## Coating and protection

- External mill varnish or rust-preventive coating for transit and storage
- Thread compound on pin and coupling threads; storage or running compound per client specification
- Pressed steel or composite thread protectors on both ends
- Internal coating and internal plastic coating (IPC) on request

## Marking

Stencil and die-stamp marking per API 5CT, including manufacturer identification, specification, size, weight, grade, process, PSL, hydrostatic test pressure and heat number. Colour-band identification per grade. Additional client marking (PO number, item number, joint number) on request.

## Product specification levels

- **PSL-1** — baseline API 5CT requirements.
- **PSL-2** — additional requirements for chemistry, toughness and inspection.
- **PSL-3** — the most stringent requirements, including additional NDT and testing.

The PSL is stated on the purchase order and confirmed on the mill test certificate.
