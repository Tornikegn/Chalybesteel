## Pup joints

Short-length casing and tubing joints for spacing out strings and positioning downhole equipment. Typical lengths of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12 ft; other lengths per order. Machined from API 5CT pipe or bar stock in the grade of the string.

## Couplings

Replacement and spare couplings for STC, LTC, BTC, NUE and EUE connections, including regular, special clearance and special bevel types. Supplied with mill test certificates and colour-band identification per grade.

## Crossovers

Crossover subs between different sizes, weights or connection types, manufactured to drawing or client specification. Connection threading performed by licensed threaders where proprietary connections are involved.

## How to order

Send the size, weight, grade, connection on each end, length and quantity through the RFQ form. Accessories can be quoted with a casing or tubing order so that grades, connections and documentation match.
