## Overview

We supply seamless tubing manufactured to API 5CT / ISO 11960 for production, injection and workover strings. Non-upset (NUE) and external-upset (EUE) tubing is available across Group 1–3 grades, with sour-service grades qualified to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 on request.

Tubing is sourced from mills selected for the required size, grade and PSL, and every order ships with mill test certificates and the inspection records agreed at quotation.

## End finish

- Threaded and coupled (T&C), coupling power-tight on one end
- Plain end (PE) or upset plain end on request
- Special clearance couplings and special bevel couplings on request

## Coating and protection

- External mill varnish or rust-preventive coating
- Thread compound on pin and coupling threads
- Thread protectors on both ends
- Internal plastic coating (IPC) on request for corrosive or injection service

## Marking

Stencil and die-stamp marking per API 5CT, including manufacturer identification, specification, size, weight, grade, upset type, process, PSL, hydrostatic test pressure and heat number. Colour-band identification per grade. Additional client marking on request.

## Product specification levels

- **PSL-1** — baseline API 5CT requirements.
- **PSL-2** — additional requirements for chemistry, toughness and inspection.
- **PSL-3** — the most stringent requirements, including additional NDT and testing.
